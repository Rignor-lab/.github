
# Rignor Lab

**Independent technology. Practical ideas. Built to last.**

Rignor Lab is an independent technology organization focused on building
software, AI systems, developer tools, and practical technology products.

We take ideas from early experimentation to reliable, usable products.

## What We Build

- AI-powered applications and platforms
- Software and web applications
- Developer tools and libraries
- APIs and backend systems
- Automation and infrastructure
- Experimental and research projects

## Our Approach

We focus on practical engineering, thoughtful design, and technology that
solves real problems.

Projects may begin as experiments, evolve through open-source development,
and eventually become production-ready products.

## Open Source

We believe useful technology should be shared when possible.

Public repositories include their own documentation, contribution guidelines,
and licensing information.

## Collaboration

We welcome developers, designers, researchers, and builders interested in
creating useful technology.

For project-specific discussions, please use the issues or discussions
available in the relevant repository.
