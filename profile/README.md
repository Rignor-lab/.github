## About us

Rignor Labs is a software and AI company based in Bengaluru, India, founded in 2021. We build scalable, secure, and high-performance digital products for education, healthcare, hospitality, NGOs, media, and cultural institutions.

What makes us different is that we combine mainstream engineering with real Tibetan language and cultural expertise. Projects that need linguistic accuracy and cultural context are handled by people who understand both the technology and the community it serves.

> *Innovation rooted in tradition, technology that serves humanity.*

## What we build

- **Custom software:** business management systems, admin dashboards, API integrations, scalable architectures
- **AI solutions:** chatbots, workflow automation, intelligent data processing, and NLP with Tibetan language support
- **Mobile apps:** iOS and Android, cross-platform, offline-capable for low-connectivity regions
- **Web platforms:** responsive, SEO-optimized sites, e-commerce, CMS, and progressive web apps
- **Consulting:** MVP development, architecture reviews, performance optimization, Salesforce support
- **Cultural technology:** Tibetan script digitization, language learning apps, multilingual content systems, digital archiving
